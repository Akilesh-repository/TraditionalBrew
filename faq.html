Tasting Room & Tours

Walk-ins are welcome for our standard tasting flights. However, for private cellar tours and guided omakase pairings, we highly recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance to secure your spot.
Our guided cellar tours typically last around 45 to 60 minutes. This includes a walkthrough of our historic brewing facilities, a detailed explanation of the brewing process, and concludes with a guided tasting session.
While the tasting room is strictly restricted to visitors of legal drinking age (20+ in Japan), minors accompanied by an adult are welcome to join the historical and educational portion of the brewery tour.
We offer small artisanal snacks like local cheese, cured meats, and crackers that pair perfectly with our sake. However, we do not operate a full-service restaurant. We can recommend excellent local dining options nearby!
Yes, our main tasting hall, retail shop, and the first floor of our historical kura are fully wheelchair accessible. Please let us know in advance if you require any specific accommodations during your tour.

Purchasing & Shipping

Yes, we partner with specialized international couriers to ship our sake to over 30 countries worldwide. Shipping rates and customs regulations vary depending on the destination country.
Standard pasteurized sake should be stored upright in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. Unpasteurized sake (Namazake) is highly sensitive and must be kept refrigerated at all times.
Due to the delicate and perishable nature of our products, we cannot accept returns on opened bottles. If a bottle arrives damaged or compromised, please contact our support team within 48 hours for a replacement.
Generally, unopened pasteurized sake should be consumed within 10 to 12 months of the brewing date printed on the label. Once opened, we recommend finishing the bottle within 2 to 3 weeks for optimal flavor.
Yes! We offer special pricing and customizable labels for weddings, corporate events, and large parties. Please reach out to our wholesale team via the Contact page to discuss your event requirements.
