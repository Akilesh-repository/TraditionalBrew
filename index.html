The Art of Sake

Mastering the Elements.

Every drop of TraditionalBrew sake begins with pristine local water and meticulously polished rice. Our Kurabito (brewers) dedicate their lives to this sensory craft.

Seimai (Polishing)

We carefully polish our Yamadanishiki rice to remove the outer layers, exposing the pure starchy core essential for premium Ginjo flavors.

Koji Making

The heart of the brewery. In a temperature-controlled cedar room, we cultivate Koji mold on steamed rice to convert starches into fermentable sugars.

Shikomi (Fermentation)

Yeast, koji, rice, and fresh spring water are combined in large vats. The mash ferments slowly at low temperatures for deep, complex aromatics.

Our Heritage

Dedication.

Founded over 130 years ago, TraditionalBrew remains a family-owned kura (brewery). We honor the ancestral techniques passed down through five generations of Toji (master brewers), blending ancient wisdom with modern precision to craft sake of unparalleled quality.
