Brewing Philosophy

Harmony of
Nature & Craft.

At TraditionalBrew, we believe that exceptional sake cannot be rushed. It requires an intimate understanding of the elements, unwavering patience, and a deep respect for the microbial life that transforms simple rice into a profound sensory experience.

Pristine Mountain Water

Sourced from our private underground spring, naturally filtered through granite for a soft, clean profile.

Estate-Grown Rice

Cultivating premium Yamada Nishiki rice in local paddies to ensure unparalleled starch density and quality.

Patient Fermentation

Utilizing ultra-cold, prolonged fermentation cycles to extract the most delicate, floral esters.

What to Expect on a Tour.

Join our guided brewery tours to experience the sounds, scents, and flavors of an authentic 130-year-old active sake kura.

Heritage Briefing

Step 02

Cellar Walkthrough

Walk among the giant cedar fermentation vats and witness the active brewing process.

Step 03

The Koji Muro

Peek into the sacred, cedar-lined hot room where our crucial Koji mold is meticulously cultivated.
